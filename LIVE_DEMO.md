# 🤖 RAGnaBot - AI Document Chat

**🚀 [LIVE DEMO](https://ragnabot.netlify.app/) 🚀**

RAGnaBot is an intelligent document chatbot powered by Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). Upload your documents and have natural conversations with an AI that understands your content!

## ✨ Key Features
- 📄 **Document Upload**: PDF, DOCX, TXT, Markdown support
- 🤖 **AI Chat**: Natural language Q&A with your documents  
- 🔍 **Smart Search**: Vector-based semantic document retrieval
- 💬 **Real-time**: Streaming responses with chat history
- 🔒 **Secure**: JWT authentication and user management

## 🎯 Perfect For
- Research and document analysis
- Knowledge base creation
- Educational content exploration
- Business document insights
