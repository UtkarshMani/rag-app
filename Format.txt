Summer Training Report Format
Title: Training Report on [Training Topic]

1. Introduction
•	Objective of the Training
•	Duration and Location
•	Participants

2. Training Content
•	Topics Covered
•	Training Methods (lectures, workshops, hands-on)

3. Training Schedule
•	Detailed Schedule (Date, Time, Topic, Trainer)

4. Key Learnings
•	Summary of Important Points
•	Skills Acquired

5. Feedback
•	Participant Feedback
•	Trainer Feedback/ Certificate with Feedback

6. Conclusion
•	Overall Assessment
•	Future Training Recommendations

7. Appendices
•	Additional Materials (Handouts, Presentations)
